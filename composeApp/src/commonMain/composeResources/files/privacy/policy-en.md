# Story Puzzle Privacy Policy

Updated: October 5, 2026
Effective: October 5, 2026
Version: 1.0

## 1. Who We Are and Scope

Story Puzzle ("the App") is a puzzle app designed for children and shared parent-child reading. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information, and the rights of children and their guardians.

The App is operated by the Fancy2110 team ("we"). Guardians should read this policy together with children and make a choice after fully understanding it.

## 2. Information We Collect

The core puzzle experience does not require an account. We do not ask children to provide their name, phone number, photo location, or other similar information to use the basic features.

Only after a guardian taps "Agree" do we collect the following anonymous or de-identified information through an analytics SDK, to understand how the App performs:

- Device information: model, operating system version, screen size, device language and region;
- Device identifiers used for statistics (such as an Android device identifier or the iOS IDFV), never linked to a child's real identity;
- App information: app version, network type, page views, button taps, game starts, progress, and time spent;
- Errors and performance: crashes and loading times, used for troubleshooting.

We do not collect photo library, contacts, microphone, precise location, or information about children's activities outside the App. The App contains no advertising, and information is never used for ad targeting.

## 3. Third-Party SDK

For app statistics and performance monitoring, we use the Umeng Analytics SDK (U-App).

- Provider: Umeng Tongxin (Beijing) Technology Co., Ltd.
- Purpose: installs, activity, page views, feature usage, and crashes
- Information collected: device identifiers, device model, operating system, network type, region, app version, and interaction events
- SDK privacy policy: https://www.umeng.com/page/policy

This SDK is initialized only after a guardian consents. Declining consent does not affect the puzzle features.

## 4. Storage, Sharing, and Protection

Analytics information is transmitted with encryption and stored in secure server environments for no longer than necessary for the stated purposes, after which it is deleted or anonymized.

We never sell children's personal information. Except where required by law or necessary to protect the security of the App, we do not share personally identifiable information with third parties.

We use reasonable measures such as access controls and encrypted transmission to protect information. Please understand that no internet transmission is completely secure; if a security incident occurs, we will provide notice as required by law.

## 5. Special Protection for Children

The App is designed primarily for children. Following the principle of data minimization, we commit to the following:

- Obtain a guardian's explicit consent before collecting any device information;
- Never request personal information unrelated to the puzzles;
- Never build profiles of children or conduct targeted marketing;
- Allow guardians to withdraw consent at any time, after which we stop collecting new information.

Guardians who wish to access, correct, or delete a child's information, or object to our processing, may contact us using the details below. We will respond promptly after verifying identity.

## 6. Your Rights

Guardians have the right to:

- Understand how we process a child's personal information;
- Access, correct, or delete that information;
- Withdraw previously given consent;
- Request deletion or an explanation of our processing rules.

Exercising these rights will not result in unfair treatment.

## 7. Policy Updates

We may update this policy when features or data practices change. For material changes, such as new types of information or new SDKs, we will ask for guardian consent again within the App. Continued use means acceptance of the updated policy.

## 8. Contact Us

If you have questions, comments, or complaints about this policy or children's privacy, please contact us:

- Email: support@fancy2110.com
- Please include "Story Puzzle privacy" in your message

We will reply as soon as possible.
